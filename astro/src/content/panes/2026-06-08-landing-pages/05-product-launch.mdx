## Why it converts

<Callout type="success" title="Urgency + value anchoring">
For an existing audience the page leans on <strong>scarcity</strong> (countdown,
"88 left") and <strong>price anchoring</strong> (struck-through $499 → $199, with
itemized "value" totaling far more than the price). Live counters ("312 sold
today") manufacture momentum. The deadline forces the decision <em>now</em>.
</Callout>

| Element | Conversion job |
| --- | --- |
| Countdown timer | Hard deadline → acts now, not "later" |
| Struck-through anchor price | Makes $199 feel like a steal |
| Itemized value stack | Justifies the price with math |
| "88 left / 312 sold today" | Scarcity + social momentum |

<Callout type="warning" title="Use honestly">
Fake countdowns and ever-resetting "X left" counters torch trust (and in some
places are illegal). Scarcity converts hardest when it's <strong>real</strong>.
</Callout>
