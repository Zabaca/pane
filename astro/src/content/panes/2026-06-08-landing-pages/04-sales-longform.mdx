## Why it converts

<Callout type="success" title="Persuasion stacked in order">
Cold/high-ticket buyers need convincing, so the page <strong>earns the ask</strong>:
problem → agitate the pain → show the mechanism → prove it (testimonial + hard
numbers) → only then price. The <strong>guarantee</strong> removes the final
objection right at the pricing decision.
</Callout>

| Section | Conversion job |
| --- | --- |
| Problem-first hero | Visitor self-identifies ("that's me") |
| Agitation bullets | Raises the cost of doing nothing |
| 3-step mechanism | Makes the solution feel easy & real |
| Testimonial + stats | Proof from a peer + hard data |
| Guarantee at pricing | Neutralizes risk at the moment of decision |
