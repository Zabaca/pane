## Why it converts

<Callout type="success" title="Friction is the enemy here">
Cold traffic won't read. So the page asks for <strong>one thing</strong> (email),
shows <strong>one promise</strong>, and adds a <strong>reason to act now</strong>
("50% off for life" + a live counter). The counter doubles as social proof and
scarcity. Dark, high-contrast design keeps the single CTA unmissable.
</Callout>

| Element | Conversion job |
| --- | --- |
| Email-only field | Lowest-possible commitment |
| Live signup counter | Social proof + FOMO in one number |
| "Founding members 50% off" | Incentive to act now, not later |
| No nav, no footer links | Zero exits — one path forward |

<Callout type="warning" title="The one rule">
A lead-magnet page should have <strong>no navigation</strong>. Every link that
isn't the CTA is a leak. Notice this mockup has none.
</Callout>
