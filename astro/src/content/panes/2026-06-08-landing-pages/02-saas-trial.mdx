## Why it converts

<Callout type="success" title="Conversion principles in play">
<strong>One promise, one CTA color.</strong> Indigo is reserved for the primary
action everywhere — nothing competes. <strong>Risk reversal</strong> ("no credit
card") sits directly under the button where hesitation happens. <strong>Proof
above the fold</strong> (logos) borrows trust before asking. The CTA repeats at the
end so scrollers don't have to scroll back.
</Callout>

| Element | Conversion job |
| --- | --- |
| Benefit headline (not feature) | Answers "what's in it for me" in 1s |
| Single dominant CTA, repeated | Removes choice paralysis |
| "No credit card" micro-copy | Lowers perceived risk at the click |
| Logo bar | Social proof before the pitch |
