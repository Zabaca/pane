## How to choose

| Archetype | Best traffic | Primary ask | Conversion lever |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS free-trial | Warm, intent-driven | Start trial / book demo | Clear value + low risk |
| Waitlist / lead magnet | Cold, top-of-funnel | Email only | Curiosity + tiny commitment |
| Long-form sales | Cold, high-ticket | Buy / apply | Proof + objection handling |
| Product launch | Existing audience | Buy now | Scarcity + urgency |

<Callout type="info" title="The shared conversion skeleton">
Every variant follows the same bones: <strong>one clear promise</strong> above the
fold → <strong>proof</strong> (logos, numbers, testimonials) → <strong>one dominant
CTA</strong> repeated. They differ in how much persuasion sits between the promise
and the ask.
</Callout>
